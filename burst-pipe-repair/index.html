A burst pipe is one of the most common water damage events a property can face, and potentially one of the most destructive. One broken line can put out 4 to 8 gallons every minute, so 240 to 480 gallons an hour may be running through floors, ceilings and walls. The trigger may be a hard freeze, corroded or aging lines, high pressure or physical damage, but the need is the same: act immediately so water does less harm to the structure, the floors, your possessions and the building's systems.

Novi Restoration provides 24/7 emergency response for burst pipe water damage throughout Novi and surrounding areas. Our restoration technicians can help you locate and shut off your water supply, and we coordinate with licensed plumbers so the plumbing repair is done properly. With the source under control, our crew goes straight to extraction, using truck-mounted machines and portable extractors to clear standing water from each affected area quickly.

Following extraction, our structural drying process addresses all moisture-affected materials, including drywall, insulation, wood framing, subflooring and finished flooring. Since water runs downhill through ceiling spaces, floor assemblies and wall cavities, a burst pipe frequently affects several rooms on more than one level. We trace where the water migrated with thermal cameras and moisture detection tools, find each affected area and dry it properly, heading off the mold and structural decay that follow hidden moisture.

Maintenance Tips for Burst Pipe Repair

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • A plumbing inspection by a licensed pro every few years catches corrosion and wear early
  • Wrap any bare pipe that runs through a crawl space, garage or attic without heat
  • Know where your main water shutoff valve is and how to operate it quickly
  • Install water leak detection sensors near water heaters and main supply lines
  • Check water pressure with a gauge, since anything over 80 PSI puts stress on fittings and pipes
  • Open sink cabinets and leave a slow drip running on cold nights, which helps keep pipes from freezing

Signs It Is Time for Burst Pipe Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Rusty or discolored water coming from your taps
  • Water charges that jump for no clear reason, a sign of a concealed leak
  • Faucets and showers across the building suddenly running weak
  • Unexplained standing water along baseboards or in the middle of a room
  • Hissing or rushing noises in the walls with every tap turned off
  • Faucets that give only a trickle, or nothing, during a freeze
  • A spray or steady drip coming off pipework you can see

What a Professional Brings to Burst Pipe Repair

The damage from a burst pipe tends to reach well past the wet areas in plain view. Water travels through wall cavities, along electrical conduits, through floor assemblies and between ceiling joists, saturating materials in places you may not discover for days or weeks. Hidden spaces may hold established mold by the time you notice, with structural materials seriously weakened as well. Professional restoration technicians use thermal imaging cameras and moisture meters to trace water migration and identify all affected areas immediately.

The machines we use for extraction and drying are in another class from anything sold for home use. A truck-mounted unit clears water at hundreds of times the speed of a shop vac, while a commercial dehumidifier takes 10 to 20 times more moisture out of the air than a household model. With only 24 to 48 hours before mold starts, you need equipment this fast and this strong.
