Typical Water Damage Problems in Kalamazoo

Some water problems need a phone call this minute, and others can safely wait until morning. The five below are ones we see constantly in Kalamazoo, listed from most pressing to least.

Bursts: Nothing is more time-critical than a broken water line, which keeps pouring until the supply is cut. Cold weather, depending on the season, is one trigger, with the leak appearing as the frozen section thaws. Close the main valve, open a faucet to relieve pressure and call right away, even at night. If a ceiling is sagging from the weight of water, stay out from under it and do not poke it.

Runoff: Storm water coming under a door or through a garage is next in line. There is no valve to close here, so lift what you can off the floor and avoid wet electrical outlets. Having passed over pavement and soil on the way in, it carries dirt and germs. The Novi Restoration team extracts it once conditions are safe, then removes soaked materials that cannot be cleaned.

Kitchens: A dishwasher that leaks from its door or a refrigerator line that drips behind the unit calls for same-day attention. Turning off the supply valve for that appliance normally ends the leak itself. What remains is the water trapped below the cabinets and floor covering, and it will not dry by itself. In Kalamazoo kitchens we often dry cabinets in place by sending air through small holes in the toe kick.

Crawlspaces: Moisture under a raised floor is seldom an emergency, but it should not be put off for a season. When we look underneath, we tend to find water lying on the liner, insulation that is soaked or sweating ducts. Given time, that humidity works its way upstairs and the joists start to rot. Our crew clears any standing water, brings the framing down to a normal moisture level and reports the likely cause.

Odors: Last on the list is a stale, earthy odor in a room that once had a leak, which is worth a scheduled visit. Mold can begin within 24 to 48 hours of a material getting wet, so a leak from months ago had plenty of time. Do not scrub or spray a large patch yourself, because disturbing it spreads spores through the house. Our technicians test the area, contain it and remove the growth along with the moisture feeding it across Kalamazoo.

Preventing Water Damage in Kalamazoo Homes

A home usually gives a few hints before a leak turns serious. The signs are easy to miss if you do not know them, so here is what our technicians look for in Kalamazoo homes. Blistering paint, ceiling stains, warped baseboards and floorboards that curl at the edges each point to water behind or beneath them. Add a stale odor in an enclosed space, a loose toilet, or a faucet that keeps dripping.

Taken singly they are minor, though every one of them gets more expensive with time. Renew the caulk around baths and showers once it splits, because water that slips behind tile stays hidden for a long time. If you have a sump pump, pour a bucket of water into the pit a few times a year to confirm it switches on. Keep stored items off the basement or garage floor on shelves or in plastic bins.

No amount of maintenance prevents every leak. Fewer and smaller leaks are still a real gain, especially compared with a Kalamazoo home full of drying machines for most of a week.
