Whether the water came from a storm, an overflowing river, a backed-up sewer, a failed sump pump or a burst plumbing line, flood damage needs a trained crew right away. In nearly every case the water falls into Category 2, known as gray water, or Category 3, known as black water, which means contaminants, bacteria and other hazards that are a real health risk. Day or night, 24/7, Novi Restoration sends flood cleanup crews throughout Novi to remove floodwater, sanitize the affected rooms and safely restore your home or building.

We start with fast water extraction, using truck-mounted and submersible pumps that can move thousands of gallons per hour. Next our technicians, who hold IICRC certification, judge the level of contamination and choose a sanitizing plan that covers HEPA air filtration, antimicrobial application and disinfecting each surface that got wet. Anything unsalvageable comes out and is hauled off correctly, including wet insulation, drywall that took in contaminated water and waterlogged carpet padding.

Then the drying begins: high-velocity air movers and commercial dehumidifiers of the LGR (low-grain refrigerant) type go in to pull moisture out of the building's structural parts. We check moisture levels every day with calibrated meters and thermal imaging so the drying is complete and mold does not get a start. Your insurance adjuster will want scope-of-work reports, equipment logs, moisture maps and photographs, so we keep that documentation going through the whole project.

Maintenance Tips for Flood Cleanup

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Use waterproof bins and raised shelving for anything valuable kept at ground level or below
  • Seal foundation cracks and apply a waterproofing coating to basement walls
  • A backwater valve on your sewer line keeps a backup from pushing sewage indoors
  • Being outside a designated flood zone does not rule out flooding, so think about flood coverage
  • Make sure the ground slopes away from your foundation on every side
  • Elevate appliances and electrical systems in flood-prone parts of your property

When to Call for Flood Cleanup in Novi

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Receding water that leaves muddy residue and debris behind
  • Any amount of water sitting in a room, garage or basement
  • Storm damage that lets rain in through the roof or walls
  • Ground so saturated that puddles sit against the foundation walls
  • Water coming in through foundation cracks, window wells or door thresholds
  • Basement flooding after the sump pump quit in a downpour
  • Stains along baseboards and lower walls that mark the flood level

The Benefits of Professional Flood Cleanup

Getting the standing water out is only one part of cleaning up after a flood. Floodwater carries bacteria, sewage, chemicals and other contaminants that are serious health hazards. Protecting your family from E. coli, hepatitis and similar waterborne illness takes proper containment, correct use of protective equipment (PPE) and proven sanitizing methods, all things professional teams train for.

Improper cleanup also tends to miss moisture you cannot see, and within days that trapped water can cause a major mold problem. With thermal imaging, psychrometric calculations and moisture meters, trained technicians confirm that subfloors, structural framing, wall cavities and every other wet area dry to an acceptable standard. Drying by measurement is the one dependable method for avoiding secondary damage, which can add thousands to the repair bill.
