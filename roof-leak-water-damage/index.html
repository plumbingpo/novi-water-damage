Roof Leak Water Damage in Novi: What to Know

Where you see the stain and where the rain gets in are usually two different places. A loose shingle, a failed flashing joint or an aging seal around a skylight lets rain in, and it travels across the roof deck and framing until it falls into the insulation. By the time a brown ring shows on the ceiling, the attic above it has often been wet for a while. We find the full wet area and dry it out.

To be clear, roof repair is not what we do, water damage is. Inside the home, our team takes out saturated insulation, dries the wood and drywall, treats any mold and repairs the ceiling. When the roof is still letting water through, a temporary tarp is something we can install, and we will coordinate with your roofer so the repair comes before the ceiling is closed.

Right Now

Your First Steps

  1. Shut Off Wet CircuitsWater coming through a ceiling fan, a light or close to an outlet means that circuit should be turned off at the panel, and the fixture should not be touched.
  2. Catch the DripsPut a pan or bucket beneath each drip, and shift rugs, electronics and furniture clear of the wet area. Plastic or towels spread on the floor will keep it from soaking.
  3. Relieve a Ceiling BulgeIf the ceiling is holding a pocket of water, stand to one side and poke a small hole at the lowest point so it drains into a bucket.
  4. Leave the Roof AloneClimbing on slick shingles or crawling around a dark attic is not worth the risk. Just observe the entry point from the room below, then hand the job to a trained crew.
  5. Document What You SeeTake photos and video of the ceiling, the attic and any damaged belongings before you move or throw anything away, then call us to start drying.
Why It Happens

What Causes It

  • Corroded or loose metal flashing where the roof meets a chimney, a vent or a wall.
  • A vent pipe boot whose rubber has split from long sun exposure.
  • Storm impact, such as hail or a tree branch, breaking through the shingles.
  • Nails that have backed out of the decking and left small holes through the shingles.
  • Aging, cracked seals around a skylight frame that no longer keep water out.
If It Is Left

What the Water Damages

Rafters and Decking

Rafters, trusses and decking that get wet again and again begin to darken, swell and soften. Decay here means less strength in what carries the roof.

Ceiling Fixtures and Wiring

Recessed cans, ceiling fans and electrical boxes are low points where roof water collects and drips through. Moisture in a fixture can cause a short and later corrosion, which is why it needs inspection before use.

Inside the Walls

Water that reaches the top of a wall runs down inside it, wetting insulation and the back of the drywall. Bubbling paint or a swollen baseboard may be the only sign.

Mold in the Attic

Mold does well in an attic that is warm, dark and full of damp wood and drywall paper. Within 24 to 48 hours it can start growing, then move across the bottom of the roof deck.

Insurance

Does Insurance Cover It?

Homeowners policies often cover interior water damage when a roof leak is sudden and accidental, such as wind tearing off shingles or a limb puncturing the roof. Insurers generally turn down claims tied to wear, age or lack of upkeep, and they may handle the roof repair on different terms than the inside damage. Photos and moisture readings from us go to the adjuster, though only your policy decides coverage, and your agent can explain it.

Pricing

What Drives the Price

  • The ceiling texture and paint that the repair has to match.
  • How easy the attic is to reach, how high the ceilings are, and if the roof needs a tarp before work begins.
  • Drywall removal: how much of the ceiling and walls is beyond saving.
  • How far down the leak traveled, from the attic to the walls and floor.
